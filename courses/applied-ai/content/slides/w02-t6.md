## An Interface, Not a Surface

Voice is a way in and a way out of a surface you already use.

Same model, same memory, same limits. A spoken prompt still needs a goal, context, and constraints.

---

## Two Ways to Use Your Voice

| Voice mode | Dictation |
|---|---|
| A spoken conversation | Speech becomes typed text |
| Replies read aloud | You read and send it yourself |
| Mainly on phones | Almost everywhere |

---

## Voice Mode

**Good for**
- Thinking out loud
- Practicing explanations and interviews
- Being quizzed
- Hands-busy tasks

**Weaker for**
- Precise instructions
- Code, numbers, and names
- Results you need to copy or cite


---

## Dictation, Three Ways

- **In the AI app**: the microphone button in the message box
- **In your operating system**: Windows key + H, or Fn twice on a Mac
- **Dictation services** such as Wispr Flow: AI cleanup in any app

Speaking is three to four times faster than typing, so detailed prompts get easier.


---

## Check the Transcript

- Names, terms, and numbers go wrong: "sixty" becomes "sixteen"
- AI cleanup makes mistakes look finished
- Type anything that has to be exact

---

## Talk First, Type Second

1. Describe a messy idea out loud for five minutes
2. Ask for a written summary of what you said
3. Continue in text
