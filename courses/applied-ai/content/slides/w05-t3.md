## File and Document Creation

From chat, you can produce:

- Reports (Word, PDF, Markdown)
- Spreadsheets
- Slide decks
- Diagrams
- Simple interactive pages

In Claude, work shown beside the chat is an **artifact**.

---

## Describing a File

- **Content**: what should it be built from? Your analysis, your notes, these sources?
- **Audience and purpose**: who opens it, and what do they do with it?
- **Structure**: the sections, slides, or columns you want
- **Length**: pages, slides, or rows
- **Look**, where it matters: a template, colors, or a style to match

Agree on an outline first. Then ask for the file.

---

## A Finished Look Hides Errors

A polished file looks done, so it gets less scrutiny than a rough draft. Check:

- The **numbers** against the analysis they came from
- The **charts**: the right data, clear labels, an honest axis
- The **claims**, including ones added as filler to round out a section
- The **file itself**, opened in the real application, not just the preview

## Multimodal Input

The model can read:

- **Screenshots**: "What's wrong with this error?"
- **Charts**: "What does this graph claim?"
- **Scanned PDFs and photos**
- **Handwritten notes**

Check its reading of numbers and small text. A misread digit looks just as confident as a correct one.

---

## Where Image Reading Slips

- Small or blurry text
- Numbers read off a chart
- Dense tables
- Handwriting
- Counting and position
- Long PDFs: charts and scans may be skipped

Crop screenshots before you upload them.

## Generative Media

- Image generation tools create pictures from a text description. Video generation works the same way, in brief.
- **Claude does not generate raster images natively**, so this week we use another tool for photos and illustrations.
- Claude can make SVG graphics, charts, and diagrams as code, which you can edit afterward.

Pick the tool by what you need: an editable diagram, or a picture.

---

## Describing an Image

Subject · setting · composition · style · mood · use

> A wide photograph of a nurse in blue scrubs at a hospital workstation at night, seen from the side. Soft light from the screen. Leave the left third dark and empty for a slide title.

Then check it: hands, text in the image, and whether it matches the real thing.

---

## Provenance and Labeling

- Label AI-generated images as AI-generated wherever you use them.
- Watermarks and provenance data help viewers tell, but they can be stripped.
- **Deepfakes** are realistic fakes of real people, and can do real harm.
- Don't generate images of real people without their consent.

---

## Copyright and Bias

- **Copyright**: questions about training data, imitating a style, and who owns the output are unsettled, and the answers vary by country.
- **Bias**: ask for "a CEO" or "a nurse" and see who you get. Look at what you got, and describe what you actually want.

Check the tool's terms and your employer's policy before you publish generated media.
