    "Pick next token" draws one of the listed tokens at random, weighted by its probability, so a 97% token wins almost every time and a 9% token wins now and then. You can also click a token to choose it yourself. The numbers are made up, each token here is a whole word, and the draw only uses the tokens shown. A real model scores about 100,000 tokens at every step. The Next-Token Sampler demo adds temperature and other settings.
