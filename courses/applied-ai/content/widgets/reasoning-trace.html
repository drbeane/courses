Thinking

These thinking steps were written for teaching. Real thinking is longer and messier, a real model doesn't label its steps, and some tools show only a summary. The token counts are rough. What carries over is the pattern: everything the model writes while thinking becomes text it reads before its next step, so a guess that has been written down can be checked.
