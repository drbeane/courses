# About This Course
### Week 1
COSC-300/500 Applied AI Essentials

---

## What This Course Is

- You'll use modern AI tools for **real professional work**, not toy examples.
- The work spans research, writing, analysis, coding, communication, and automation.
- Over the term you move from *talking to* an AI to *delegating work* to one.
- Whatever the tool produces, you check what comes back, every time.
- You'll also learn to use these tools responsibly, with attention to privacy, bias, and risk.

<!-- Open to all majors; no prerequisites. 300 and 500 share topics; 500 adds an extension to most assignments. -->

---

## How the Course Is Built

**The weeks are loosely ordered by how much autonomy the AI has.**

1. Chat With an AI
2. Ground It in Sources and Files
3. Let It Act on Your Computer
4. Have It Build Software
5. Build With It Directly
6. Agents That Work While You're Away

The more the AI does on its own, the more your supervision skills matter, so those skills grow alongside it.

## Course AI-Use Policy

- AI use is **expected** in this course. You'll be graded on how well you use it.
- **Disclose** which tools you used and how you used them on each assignment.
- You are responsible for everything you submit, including any errors the AI made.
- Never paste other people's private data into an AI tool.
- Every assignment includes **evidence of review** that shows what you checked, not just the output.

## The AI Use Journal

Each week you'll write a short entry that answers four questions:

- What did you **delegate** to AI this week, in this course or elsewhere?
- Which **tools or skills** from the course did you use?
- What did you **check**, and how did you check it?
- What were the **results**? What worked well, and what didn't?

In week 15, the journal becomes the raw material for a final 600-word reflection.
