## Linear algebra

**Linear algebra** is a branch of mathematics that deals with (among other things) structured collections of numbers, such as **vectors** and **matrices**. When studying linear algebra, a student learns how to use these objects to represent various ideas and phenomena, how to perform mathematical operations involving these objects, and how to use these objects to solve various types of problems. It is a rich, well-developed discipline that has applications in almost every technical field, including (but not limited to) computer science, data science, artificial intelligence, statistics, physics, and engineering.

We will not attempt to provide a full coverage of linear algebra in this course, but will touch upon a few topics from the discipline that are particularly relevant to data science and artificial intelligence. We will also discuss how to apply tools from NumPy to solve problems from linear algebra. The NumPy arrays that we have been studying recently provide a natural tool for representing the sort of objects studied in linear algebra, and the NumPy package has many tools for performing calculations related to linear algebra.
