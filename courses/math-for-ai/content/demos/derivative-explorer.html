What to notice

  1. The secant slope is rise over run between two points on the graph: (f(x₀ + h) − f(x₀)) / h. As h shrinks, the secant line turns into the tangent line and its slope settles on one number. That number is the derivative.
  2. You cannot set h to 0, because the formula would divide by zero. The derivative is the limit: the value the slopes approach.
  3. Where the function is rising, the derivative is positive. Where it is falling, the derivative is negative. The steeper the graph, the farther the derivative is from 0.
  4. At a peak or a valley the tangent line is flat and the derivative is 0. These are the critical points. Find them on "x³ − 3x" and check where the derivative graph crosses the axis.
  5. For the ball, the derivative of height is velocity. It is positive on the way up, 0 at the top, and negative on the way down.
