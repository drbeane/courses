What to notice

  1. The probabilities always sum to 1, and every one of them is above 0. Softmax never rules an option out completely.
  2. At T = 1 this is the ordinary softmax function. Lower the temperature and the top word takes nearly all of the probability. At T = 0.1 the model gives the same answer almost every time.
  3. Raise the temperature and the bars even out. At T = 4 unlikely words such as "moon" are chosen often. This is why a high temperature makes a language model more varied and more error-prone.
  4. The order of the words never changes with temperature. Only the gaps between them do.
  5. Add 2 to every score and the probabilities do not move. Softmax depends on the differences between scores, not on their size.
  6. Sample 100 words and the shares only roughly match the probabilities. Sample 10,000 and they match closely. This is the Monte Carlo idea from Week 7.
