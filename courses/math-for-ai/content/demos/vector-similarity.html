What to notice

  1. Stretch one vector without turning it. Its length and the dot product both grow, and the distance changes, but the cosine similarity stays the same. Cosine similarity depends only on direction.
  2. Make the vectors perpendicular. The dot product and the cosine similarity are both 0, whatever the lengths are.
  3. Point the vectors in opposite directions. The cosine similarity is −1, the lowest value possible.
  4. Two vectors can be far apart and still have a cosine similarity of 1, if one is a longer copy of the other. Distance and cosine similarity answer different questions.
  5. In the word list, the animals are close to one another and far from the vehicles. Nothing in the calculation knows what the words mean. The grouping comes entirely from the numbers in the embeddings.
